iViz Lab

Professor, Lab Director Steve DiPaola

Simon Fraser University in the School of Interactive Arts and Technology.


iVizLab is an interdisciplinary, human-centric AI research lab dedicated to making computational systems more attuned to human experience, grounded in biological, cognitive, and behavioral models.

The lab's research centers on AI-driven computational modeling of human expression, emotion, behavior, empathy, and creativity, spanning computer graphics-based facial and character systems as well as AI-based cognitive modeling.

Rooted in advanced AI engineering, computer graphics, data visualization, and user interface design, iVizLab draws on cognitive science, neuroscience, and the arts to explore what it means to build intelligent, expressive systems.
